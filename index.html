Strategy

A product portfolio is not a price list. It's a set of distinct solutions built around specific buyers.

Most companies at this size have the products. What they're missing is the structure that makes the products easy to buy and easy to sell, and a story every rep tells the same way.

  • Buyer fitConnect what you do well to the priorities of specific industries, verticals, and buyer roles.
  • PricingGround pricing in what buyers expect, what the alternatives cost, and the value that makes you different.
  • PackagingBundle what belongs together. Let buyers add, combine, or buy à la carte, and grow without being forced into a bigger package.
  • The pitchOne core story every rep understands and can make their own. A clear top-level message and a short overview of the major product areas, so nothing gets left off the truck.

What you get: a catalog built around your buyers, packaging that preserves what sets you apart, and a sales story with the deck to back it up. It's also the raw material the agents run on.

Agents

Agents trained on the founder's voice, creating custom sales and marketing materials at the pace of the business.

As new markets, features, and customers are added, the agents produce the materials to match. Built on your positioning, your buyers, and your language, so what they make sounds like you.

  • Marketing campaignsEmails, ads, social posts, and content that start from the approved story instead of a blank page.
  • Sales enablementDecks, proposals, and quotes drafted the way the founder would write them. The rep edits and sends.
  • Product portfolioA pricing menu and product materials that stay current as the catalog changes.
  • Your team runs themDeployed where your team already works, with the knowledge base documented and your people trained to update it.

Why here and not a dev shop: an agent is only as good as the judgment behind it. Steve spent 25 years carrying quota, running sales teams, and leading product marketing before he built his first one. The agents know what a good first email does because he put it there.

Coaching

Great salespeople don't all look alike. Each rep builds a style that feels natural and gets results.

One-on-one work on real opportunities. Identify what makes each rep effective and where they get in their own way, then build on their strengths instead of a persona that doesn't fit.

  • PreparationWalk in ready. Know the buyer, the products, and the data that make the case.
  • DeliverySpeak clearly, present with confidence, and listen closely. Get better at responding under pressure.
  • NegotiationGet customers excited, build consensus, and defend value without defaulting to discounts.
  • Tools they keepA business plan, a funnel, an account growth plan, and a technical library, each built as a Claude Project the rep runs themselves.

A different path

Early growth comes fast for tech firms. The founders are personally involved in every deal, explaining the product with clarity and enthusiasm. They sell comprehensive solutions at a premium because they believe in what they've built.

Growth means team building and delegation. The founder is no longer in every room. The marketing team approximates the founder. The sales team approximates the marketing team. By the time a message reaches a prospect, it's a copy of a copy.

Our mission is to preserve the passion and voice of the founders as the company grows, so every pitch, every post, and every piece of product marketing carries the same energy. That's the difference between growth and commoditization.

About

Steve Zeller

A sales team that sells the difference, not the discount.

Steve's formative years in sales were a fight for survival. Selling managed cloud services in the early days of AWS, he watched more than forty salespeople get hired and fired around him while he carried 70% of his company's quota. He vowed to build a system where hard-working reps could succeed.

That took him into product strategy, where he built a $50M healthcare cloud practice, and then to the executive level, where he redefined product portfolios, pricing, and alliance strategies. He has led product teams at News Corp and Cablevision, and was Head of Product Strategy at Bespin Global, where he led go-to-market for AI applications. The reps he trained along the way are now VPs and CROs in their own right.

He founded Antimatter to solve an obvious but pervasive problem. Early on, founders can convey what makes their product unique and sell it at a premium. As they scale, that product gets split into SKUs, discounted, and eventually commoditized. Antimatter brings the passion of the original story back into every sales pitch, using a modern, AI-fluent toolkit to solve a timeless challenge. The goal is a sales team that sells the difference, not the discount.
