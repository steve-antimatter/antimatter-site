Getting to

Early growth comes fast for tech firms. The founders are personally involved in every deal, explaining the product with clarity and enthusiasm. They sell comprehensive solutions at a premium because they believe in what they've built.

Getting from $5M to
$50MA different path

Growth means team building and delegation. The founder is no longer in every room. The marketing team approximates the founder. The sales team approximates the marketing team. By the time a message reaches a prospect, it's a copy of a copy.

Our mission is to preserve the passion and voice of the founders as the company grows, so every pitch, every post, and every piece of product marketing carries the same energy. That's the difference between growth and commoditization.

How it works

From one conversation to every touchpoint.

Four steps. We listen to the people who know the product best, train agents on what they say, put the agents to work, and coach the reps to deliver it.

01

Listen

It starts with a conversation. Founders and product experts, talking about why customers buy, what makes the product different, and how it should be priced.

02

Train

That conversation becomes the knowledge base: positioning, buyers, products, pricing, and the way the company talks. Agents are trained on it, so what they produce sounds like you.

03

Produce

The agents do the work. Campaigns, sales decks, content, and product materials, created in the founder's voice at the pace of the business.

04

Coach

Then we coach the reps to present it. One-on-one work on real deals, so every rep tells the story with the founder's conviction and their own style.

Strategy

A product portfolio is not a price list. It's a set of distinct solutions built around specific buyers.

A

Industry and buyer fit

Connect what you do well to the priorities of specific industries, verticals, and buyer roles. Solutions that speak their language.

B

Market-based pricing

Ground pricing in what buyers expect, what the alternatives cost, and the value that makes you different.

C

Bundles that scale

Bundle what belongs together. Let buyers add, combine, or buy à la carte, and grow without being forced into a bigger package.

2 · AI agents for marketing and sales

Agents

Agents trained on the founder's voice that create custom sales and marketing materials at the pace of the business, as new markets, features, and customers are added.

Why it works here and not from a dev shop

An agent is only as good as the judgment behind it. A developer can build you an agent that writes emails. It will write emails that sound like a developer thinks a salesperson sounds.

I spent 25 years carrying quota, running sales teams, and leading product marketing before I built my first agent. I know what a good first email does and what a bad one costs. The agents I build know it too, because I put it there.

What you get

Working agents, deployed where your team already works. The positioning, buyer, and product knowledge they run on, documented, so it can be updated when the company changes. Your team trained to use them and to edit the knowledge base themselves.

Nothing goes out without a human reading it.

3 · Sales team coaching

Coaching

Great salespeople don't all look alike. The job is to help each rep find a style that feels natural, earns the customer's confidence, and gets results.

Identify what makes each rep effective and where they get in their own way. Then help them use their strengths instead of overcompensating with too much talking, forced technical language, or a persona that doesn't fit.

Preparation

Walk in ready. Know the buyer, the products, and the data that make the case.

Delivery

Speak clearly, present with confidence, and listen closely. Get better at responding under pressure.

Negotiation

Get customers excited, build consensus, and defend value without defaulting to discounts or giveaways.

Tools they keep

A business plan, a funnel, an account growth plan, and a technical library, each built as a Claude Project the rep runs themselves. The coaching ends. The tools don't.
